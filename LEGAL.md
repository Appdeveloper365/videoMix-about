## 2. Privacy Policy

1. **Local-first.** In local sidecar mode, every download, probe, mix and file
   operation runs entirely on your own device. Nothing leaves your machine.
2. **No telemetry.** videoMix contains no analytics, tracking, advertising,
   crash reporting or background data harvesting.
3. **Remote mode.** If you connect to a remote server, media and job metadata
   are transmitted only to the server *you* configure — typically your own
   home server, LAN host or private network — and between your device and it,
   nowhere else.
4. **Data retention.** Downloads, uploads and mixed outputs are stored
   temporarily in the backend's local data directory on the machine running the
   backend, solely to serve your work, until deleted (manually or by
   housekeeping).
5. **Secrets stay local.** API keys and connection settings never leave your
   device; the app stores them only in local application storage.
6. **Cookies.** If you supply browser cookies or a `cookies.txt` file, they are
   used only for your own authenticated access to the sources you choose, and
   are kept on the machine running the backend.

**Summary:** your media never reaches the developers of videoMix. There is no
developer-run service in the loop.
