Introduction

Paste a video link and videoMix inspects it live, then walks you through your mix step by step:

  • 🎵 Blend in a soundtrack — replace the audio, mix with independent volumes, or auto-ducking that dips the music when speech plays.
  • 🎸 Extract audio only — MP3, M4A, FLAC or WAV.
  • ✂️ Trim exactly — seconds-precise start and end on a live timeline.
  • 🎚️ Fast Copy or full re-encode — instant quality-preserving copy, or H.264/265 with CRF and hardware acceleration.
  • 📱 Yours everywhere — desktop, browser, and your own home server. Local-first: nothing is sent to anyone but your machine.
