# videoMix

**videoMix** is a free video and audio mixing application. Paste a link (YouTube, TikTok, X, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, or a direct media file), and the app inspects it live, then walks you through a mix step by step.

## What it does

- **🎵 Blend a soundtrack into a video** — three modes: replace the original audio, mix alongside it with independent volume sliders, or auto-ducking (the video's speech automatically dips the background music).
- **🎸 Extract audio only** — convert any source to MP3, M4A, FLAC or WAV.
- **✂️ Trim exactly** — pick start and end seconds on a live timeline before anything is downloaded.
- **🎚️ Full control** — volume levels (0–200%), millisecond sync delay, fade in/out, and two quality pipelines: instant *Fast Copy* (no re-encode) or a full re-encode (H.264/H.265, CRF quality slider, hardware acceleration).
- **📱 Anywhere** — a desktop app (macOS/Windows/Linux), a web app in the browser, and phone-friendly remote access to your own home server. All processing happens on *your* machine or *your* server — nothing is sent to us.
